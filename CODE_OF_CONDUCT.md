# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated. Report concerns privately to the maintainer via a GitHub security/contact channel on this repository; the maintainer will review and may remove content or contributors who violate these norms.
(This is a short project statement, not a full adoption of the Contributor Covenant text; adopt the full text before accepting community contributions at scale.)
